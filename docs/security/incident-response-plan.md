# TikRadar Incident Response Plan

Version 1.0 — 2026-08-26.

1. **Report and triage:** record time, reporter, affected service and suspected data without copying secrets into the report.
2. **Contain:** disable affected access, revoke tokens and keys, isolate the affected workflow and preserve relevant audit events.
3. **Assess:** determine affected systems, users, data categories, duration and likelihood of harm.
4. **Eradicate and recover:** patch the cause, rotate credentials, restore validated service and monitor for recurrence.
5. **Notify:** notify TikTok Shop, affected users and competent authorities when required by contract or law.
6. **Learn:** document timeline, root cause, actions, owners and deadlines. Review controls within ten business days.

Priority 1 incidents include exposed OAuth tokens/App Secrets, unauthorized production access or confirmed personal-data disclosure. They require immediate containment.
